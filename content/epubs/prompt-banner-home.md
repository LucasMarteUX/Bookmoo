# Prompt — banner da Home (chamada para a galeria)

Formato: **largura total**, proporção 4:1 (ideal 2400×600; no celular o app corta pela direita, então a ação fica à direita).
Salvar como `public/banners/gallery-home.webp` (o app já usa automaticamente quando o arquivo existir).

## Prompt (copiar)

Wide panoramic banner, 4:1 aspect ratio (2400x600), flat modern editorial illustration, no gradients, no glow, no texture, crisp clean finish. Deep ink-blue background (#14203A) with a calm, warm mood. On the RIGHT 60% of the image, a fanned row of eight standing book covers of public-domain classics, slightly overlapping, each different in color and style, all fully legible and well drawn: a green Victorian cover with a white rabbit (Alice in Wonderland), a black-and-red gothic cover with a bat and castle (Dracula), a teal sea cover with a submarine and tentacles (Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas), a golden treasure map cover with a skull flag (Treasure Island), a red-and-gold Mars landscape cover with two moons (A Princess of Mars), a yellow-brick road cover with an emerald city (The Wonderful Wizard of Oz), a rainy foggy London street cover with a lamp (Sherlock Holmes), a green gabled farmhouse with a girl with red braids (Anne of Green Gables). Covers are original artwork, no film imagery, no real people, no logos. A small sparkling bookmark ribbon in warm amber (#E8A44A) peeks from one cover. The LEFT 40% is intentionally calm and mostly empty (solid ink-blue) so text can be placed over it by the app: do NOT write any title or text there. No text anywhere except what is naturally on the book covers, spelled exactly: "ALICE", "DRACULA", "TREASURE ISLAND", "OZ". Soft long shadows under the books, shelf-free floating composition, premium, cozy, inviting.

## Variante para celular (opcional, 3:2, 1200×800)
Same style and books, but only five covers fanned at the bottom-right, empty calm ink-blue space on top and left.

## Observações
- O app escreve por cima, em branco: "Sua próxima leitura está na galeria" + botão "Explorar a galeria". Por isso a esquerda precisa ficar lisa.
- Sem a imagem, o app mostra um painel chapado com capas reais do catálogo.
