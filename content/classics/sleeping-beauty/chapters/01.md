# The Frog by the Pond

Long, long ago, in a country far away, there lived a king and a queen. They had a beautiful castle with tall towers and a big garden. They had gold and silver, fine clothes, and the best food and wine. They had horses and servants and a golden coach. Every person in the country knew the king and the queen, and everyone said, "They have everything."

But they did not have everything. They did not have a child.

Every day, the queen walked in her garden, and she looked at the flowers and the trees, and she was sad. Every night, the king sat next to her in front of the fire, and they did not say very much. They were not young anymore. In their castle, there was a big room with a little bed in it, and the bed was empty year after year.

"If only we had a child," said the queen. She said it again and again. "I would be the happiest woman in the world."

"I know," said the king, and he took her hand. "I wish it too."

One warm summer day, the queen went for a walk. She walked to the end of her garden, where there was a little pond with green water. The sun was hot, and she sat down in the shade of a tree. She was tired, and she closed her eyes.

Suddenly, she heard a soft splash. She opened her eyes. A little green frog had jumped out of the pond, and it was sitting on a stone in front of her. It looked at her with its round, bright eyes.

"Queen," said the frog, in a small, clear voice, "your wish will come true. Before a year is over, you will have a daughter."

The queen could not speak. She looked at the frog with big eyes. She did not know if she was dreaming. But the frog did not say anything more. It gave a jump, and it went back into the water with a little splash, and the circles on the pond grew wider and wider until they were gone.

The queen sat for a long time. Then she stood up, and she ran back to the castle with her skirt in her hands. She found the king in the great hall, and she told him about the frog. The king listened. At first he did not believe her. Then he saw that her eyes were bright, and that she was laughing and crying at the same time.

"Is it true?" he said.

"I think it is," said the queen. "I believe it with all my heart."

And it was true. Before the end of the year, the queen had a baby girl.

She was the most beautiful baby that anyone had ever seen. She had a small round face and soft pink cheeks. Her eyes were big and bright, and when she smiled, the whole room seemed to shine. The king looked at his daughter for a long time. He could not say a word. He was so happy that he could not stop smiling. He held her in his two big hands as if she were made of glass.

"Look at her," he said at last. "She is like a little rose."

"Yes," said the queen. "A little rose."

The king was so proud and so glad that he wanted everyone to know. "We will have a great feast," he said. "We will invite all our family. We will invite our friends and our neighbors, and all the nobles of the land. Everyone will come and see our daughter."

"And we must invite the wise women," said the queen. She spoke slowly. "In our country, there are thirteen of them. They are good and powerful women, and they know the old magic. If we ask them, they will bless our child. They will give her good gifts."

The king thought about this. "That is a fine idea," he said. "We will invite them all."

But when the servants counted the plates in the royal kitchen, they brought bad news. The king had only twelve golden plates. They were very old and very fine. Nobody could make new ones. There was no thirteenth plate for the thirteenth wise woman.

"What shall we do?" asked the queen.

The king frowned. He walked up and down the room with his hands behind his back. At last, he stopped.

"There is only one answer," he said. "We will invite twelve wise women. The thirteenth will stay at home. She is old and she lives far away. Perhaps she will not even hear about the feast."

The queen did not like this idea. But she could think of no better one. So she nodded, and the servants went out with the invitations.

None of them knew that this was a very big mistake.
