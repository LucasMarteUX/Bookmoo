# The Terrible Shark Day

Pinocchio went to school the next morning. He sat at a wooden desk, and he opened his new book. He tried very hard. He listened to the teacher, and he did his homework. In a few weeks, he was the best student in the class. The teacher said, "Pinocchio is very clever. He will be a great man one day."

But he had one problem. He had many friends, and some of them were bad boys. "Why do you study so much?" they said. "Study is boring. Come and play with us."

"I cannot," said Pinocchio. "I promised the Fairy."

"But there is something to see," said a boy named Eugene. "A big thing. A great sea monster. People say that it is the Terrible Shark. It came near the beach. Let us go and look at it."

Pinocchio wanted to see the shark. He remembered the dolphin's words. "I will go with you," he said. "But only for one hour."

They walked to the beach. It was empty. They looked at the sea, and they saw nothing but waves.

"Where is the shark?" said Pinocchio.

"There is no shark," said Eugene, and he laughed. "We lied to you. We wanted you to come and play with us."

Pinocchio was angry. "You are bad friends," he said. "I am going back to school."

"You are afraid of the teacher," said a boy. "You are a little baby."

"I am not!" said Pinocchio. He was angry. He took a book from his bag and threw it at the boys. Then the boys threw their books at him. Soon there was a big fight. They threw books, and they threw sand, and they pushed each other. Books flew in all directions.

One boy was hit on the head with a heavy book. He fell on the sand and did not move.

"He is dead!" cried the boys. They ran away in fear.

Pinocchio ran to the boy. He tried to help him. He put water on his face, and he called his name. But the boy did not wake up.

At that moment, two policemen came. They saw Pinocchio with the boy, and they said, "What have you done? You are a murderer! Come with us!" They put him in handcuffs and took him to the police station.

At the station, a man came to say that the boy was only unconscious. He opened his eyes after an hour. But Pinocchio was still in trouble. The policemen said, "You were fighting. You will go to prison."

Pinocchio was afraid. He broke away from the policemen and ran. A big dog ran after him. It was a fast hunting dog called Alidoro. Pinocchio ran along the beach, and the dog came close behind him.

At last, Pinocchio came to the edge of the sea. He jumped into the water and swam out as fast as he could. But Alidoro jumped in after him. The dog was a good swimmer.

"Oh, no," thought Pinocchio. "I will be caught. I am too tired to go on."

Suddenly, a big green fisherman came out of the water. He was a fisherman who did not look like a man. He had green skin, green hair, a green beard and eyes like a fish. He caught Pinocchio in a net with a lot of fish. "What a big, strange fish!" he said. "Today I will make a fine meal."

He took Pinocchio to his cave. He put him in a big pan of flour. "I will fry you with the others," he said. "I do not know what kind of fish you are. But you will taste good."

Pinocchio was covered with flour from head to foot. He looked like a white ghost. He was so afraid that he could not speak. The green man put the pan on the fire. The oil began to bubble.

At that moment, Alidoro came into the cave. He was wet and hungry. He smelled the fried fish, and he began to eat. The fisherman shouted. But Alidoro was faster. He took the biggest fish and ran away.

The fisherman looked at the pan. There was Pinocchio, white and shaking. He lifted him up. "A puppet!" he said. "I never saw a fish like that." He looked at the oil, and he laughed. He opened the door of the cave and put Pinocchio outside.

Alidoro was waiting. He looked at Pinocchio. Then he said, to his great surprise, "Do not be afraid. I will not hurt you. You saved my life once. I am Alidoro, and I am a friend."

"Saved your life?" said Pinocchio.

"When I was a puppy, you pulled me out of the water. You do not remember. But I remember you. Now I have paid my debt."

Pinocchio thanked him. He was very tired. He walked home to the Fairy's cottage on the hill.
