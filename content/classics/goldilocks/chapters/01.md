# The House of the Three Bears

Once upon a time, there were three bears. They lived together in a little house in the middle of a green wood. One of them was a Little, Small, Wee Bear. One of them was a Middle-sized Bear. And one of them was a Great, Huge Bear.

The three bears were a happy family. The Great, Huge Bear had a great, rough, gruff voice. When he spoke, the cups on the shelf shook a little. The Middle-sized Bear had a middle voice. It was not loud and not soft. And the Little, Small, Wee Bear had a little, small, wee voice. It was high and sweet, like a bell.

In their house, everything came in three. Each bear had his own pot for porridge. The Great, Huge Bear had a great pot. The Middle-sized Bear had a middle-sized pot. The Little, Small, Wee Bear had a little pot, and it was blue.

Each bear had his own chair, too. There was a great chair for the Great, Huge Bear, with a hard seat. There was a middle-sized chair for the Middle-sized Bear, with a soft cushion. And there was a little chair for the Little, Small, Wee Bear. It was a pretty chair, made of light wood, and it was neither too hard nor too soft.

Each bear also had his own bed in the room upstairs. There was a great bed, a middle-sized bed, and a little bed. They all had warm blankets, and they all had pillows.

The bears kept their house very clean. Every morning, they swept the floor. They washed their cups and plates. They watered the flowers in front of the door. They did not have many visitors, because the wood was very quiet. But the bears did not mind. They liked their life. They were kind, and they never did any harm to anyone. They were good bears, a little rough, as bears are, but very good-natured.

One bright morning, the three bears got up early. The sun was shining through the window, and the birds were singing in the trees. The Great, Huge Bear made the porridge. He put oats and milk in a big pot, and he stirred it on the fire. When it was ready, he poured it into the three pots, one by one: a great pot, a middle-sized pot, and a little pot.

"It is very hot," said the Great, Huge Bear, in his great, rough, gruff voice. "If we eat it now, we will burn our mouths."

"Then let us go for a walk," said the Middle-sized Bear, in his middle voice. "It is a lovely morning. When we come back, the porridge will be cool."

"Yes! Yes!" said the Little, Small, Wee Bear, in his little, small, wee voice. He jumped up and down. "I love to walk in the wood! Can we look for flowers?"

"We can look for anything you like," said the Middle-sized Bear, and he laughed.

So the three bears put on their coats. They left the porridge on the table, in the three pots, and the steam went up in the sunlight. They did not lock the door. They never locked it. They were good bears, and they did not think that anyone would harm them. They closed it softly behind them, and they set off along the path.

They walked slowly through the wood. The Little, Small, Wee Bear ran in front, and he picked up leaves and sticks. The Middle-sized Bear showed him a bird's nest. The Great, Huge Bear walked behind, and he told them the names of the trees. It was a peaceful morning.

They did not know that someone was watching the little house.
