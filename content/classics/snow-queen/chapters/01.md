# The Troll's Mirror

Listen. I am going to tell you a story. When it is finished, you will know more than you know now.

Once there was a troll. He was a bad troll, one of the worst in the world. One day, he was very happy, because he had made a mirror. It was a magic mirror. It showed the world in a strange way. Everything good and beautiful looked small and ugly in it. Everything bad looked big and terrible. The most beautiful green fields looked like boiled spinach. The kindest people looked horrible. Their faces turned strange, and no one could know them. If a man had one small spot on his nose, the mirror made it cover his whole face. And if a good thought came into a person's head, the mirror showed only an ugly smile.

The troll thought that this was great fun. Every day, he held the mirror up to the world, and he laughed.

The troll had a school for little trolls. They all came to him, and they said, "Look! Now you can see how the world and the people really are!" They ran about with the mirror until there was no country and no person that they had not seen in it.

Then the little trolls wanted to fly up to heaven. They wanted to laugh at the angels. The higher they flew with the mirror, the more it shook with laughter. They could hardly hold it. They flew higher and higher, nearer to God and the angels. Then the mirror shook so hard that it jumped out of their hands. It fell down to the earth, and it broke into millions and millions of pieces.

Now it did more harm than before. Some pieces were as small as a grain of sand. They flew all over the world. When a piece got into someone's eye, it stayed there. Then that person saw everything wrong. He saw only what was bad in things and in people, because every small piece of glass kept the power of the whole mirror. Some people got a piece in the heart. That was the worst. Their hearts became cold, like a lump of ice.

Some pieces were big. People used them as glass for their windows. But it was not good to look at your friends through those windows. Other pieces were made into glasses for the eyes. But when people put them on, to see well and to be fair, everything looked wrong.

The wicked troll laughed until his stomach hurt. And the little pieces of glass went on flying through the air. Now listen, and I will tell you what happened next.
