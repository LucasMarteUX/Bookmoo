# Three Drops of Blood

It was the middle of winter. Big, soft flakes of snow were falling from the sky, and the whole country was white and quiet. In a tall castle, a queen sat at a window and sewed. The window had a frame of black ebony wood. It was dark and smooth, and it shone like glass.

The queen was a kind woman, and she was sad. She had been married to the king for a long time, but she had no children. Every day, she hoped and waited. Every day, the cradle in her room stayed empty.

That afternoon, she looked out at the snow, and she forgot her work. Her needle slipped, and it cut her finger. Three drops of blood fell down. They fell on the white snow outside the window, and they were very red.

The queen looked at the red drops on the white snow for a long time. Then she looked at the black wood of the window. She thought of all three colors together. She felt a deep wish in her heart.

"I want a little daughter," she said quietly. "I want her to be as white as snow. I want her to be as red as blood. And I want her hair to be as black as the wood of this window."

She did not know that anyone could hear her wish. But the winter wind seemed to carry her words far away.

A few months later, the snow melted, and the garden turned green. The birds came back, and the apple trees opened their flowers. In the spring, the queen had a baby. It was a little girl, and she was beautiful. Her skin was as white as snow. Her lips and cheeks were as red as blood. Her hair was as black as ebony. The queen held her in her arms and cried happy tears.

"Her name will be Snow White," she said.

The king came into the room, and he looked at his daughter for a long time. He did not say a word. Then he smiled, and he kissed the queen on the forehead.

For some weeks, the castle was full of happiness. The servants sang in the kitchen. The cook made sweet cakes. The gardener brought roses to the queen's room. The king took his little girl in his arms and walked around the castle with her. He showed her the great hall, the long stairs, and the big windows.

But the queen was weak. She had loved her daughter from the first moment, but she was ill after the birth, and she did not get better. Every day, she became a little paler. Every day, her voice became a little softer. At last, she called the king to her bed.

"Please take good care of our daughter," she said. "Love her for both of us."

"I will," said the king, and his eyes were full of tears.

The queen smiled at him. She looked once more at the window, where the snow had fallen on that winter day. Then she closed her eyes, and she did not open them again.

The whole country was sad. People wore black clothes. The church bells rang slowly for many days. In the garden of the castle, the flowers hung their heads. The king did not smile again for a long time.

Only the baby did not know what had happened. She lay in her cradle and looked at the light on the ceiling. She laughed when the sun came through the window. When she was hungry, she cried. When she was warm and full, she slept.

The servants took care of her. They fed her and sang to her, and they carried her in the garden when the weather was good. Snow White grew quickly. At one year old, she could stand. At two years old, she could walk. At three years old, she could say many words. She was a happy, bright child, and everybody loved her.

But the king was alone. In the evenings, he sat in his big chair and looked at the fire. His house was big and quiet. He thought about his wife. He thought about his small daughter, who had no mother.

"A child needs a mother," he said to himself. "And a castle needs a queen."

So after a year, he made a decision. He did not know that it was a very bad one.
